# Guide for Better Sleep
## 1. Diet & Substances
Avoid eat large meal a few hours before sleep. Imagine your body is a factory. Before sleep, your body need to relax. If you eat large before sleep, that makes your factory working again.

Avoid drinking to much because you will want to go to restroom, and it can disrupt your sleep.

If you want to drink coffee, it is recommended 8-12 hours before sleep.

Smokes are bad for sleep.

## 2. Timing
Have a consistent sleep schedule.

## 3. Light Level
Avoid blue light as much as possible after sunset.

## 4. Environment
Make sure your bed is only used for sleep. It will help your unconscious mind to think that your bed is for sleep. Meanwhile, your other room is for activity, so your brain know when to sleep and when to focus.

Have a comfortable bed and pillow also help you sleep better.

Temperature around 18.3 C is recommended.

## 5. You
Exercise makes sleep better. Avoid doing exercise 2-3 hours before sleep.

Doing before sleep ritual that makes you relax. Some activity like reading a book or take a shower. Try to avoid activity that require blue light screen or electronic. For me reading a book for 30 minutes helps me relax.
